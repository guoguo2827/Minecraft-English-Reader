Original source SVGs used by these compositions are preserved in `sources/`.
`manifest.json` identifies the source icons and SHA-256 hashes for each file.
Images are stored locally, without remote hotlinks or runtime image requests.

Changes: icons scaled and arranged on a shared background; an original short
English example added as a caption. Examples and Chinese translations were
written for this project, not copied from a commercial textbook.

These are captioned contextual association aids, not 1400 independently drawn
literal illustrations. Some words share contextual symbols. A picture may show
only part of a sentence's setting, not the exact target object or relationship.
Do not use these images as the sole answer criterion in listening/image quizzes.
The curriculum marks all new compositions `quizSafe: false`, with visual meaning
and teacher review still pending. No endorsement by Twitter or Cambridge is implied.
